Kategra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Kategra is developed by Vartix. This page explains in plain words which information goes where while you play.

In short

  • Kategra has no server. You don't need an account, a sign-in, or to give us any information.
  • The game itself does not collect any information about you and does not send anything anywhere.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob; the ad library sends the information described below to Google.

Information that stays on your device

The following are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us or anyone else: your game progress and stars, an unfinished level, your settings (language, theme, sound, vibration), your daily free hints, and whether you bought "Remove ads". If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted too.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has three kinds of ads: a small banner below the game board and in the menus, a full-screen ad on some level transitions, and a short video when you choose to watch an ad for a hint. For this, Google's ad library (Google Mobile Ads SDK) is included in the game. According to Google, it automatically collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • IP address (may be used to estimate the general location of the device),
  • App interactions (such as app launch, taps and video views),
  • Diagnostics (performance information such as launch time, hang rate and energy use),
  • Device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID).

Google uses this information to show ads, measure ads and prevent fraud. The information is encrypted in transit (TLS).

How Google uses this information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ad content is limited to ads suitable for general audiences (G).

Your consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form when you first open it. Whether to allow personalised ads is up to you; if you don't, the game still plays fully.
  • You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details (such as your card number). The game only learns whether the purchase was made and stores that on your device.

Children

Kategra is designed for players aged 16 and over; it is not directed at children. The game itself does not collect personal information from anyone. Ads shown are limited to ads suitable for a general audience (G).

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the date above changes.
